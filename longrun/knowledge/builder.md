# Builder rules (detail)
- Prefer the smallest change that moves a criterion; commit coherent steps in the workspace branch.
- Submit evidence with the exact command and exit code; artifacts are hashed and copied by the controller.
- If blocked: `longrun observe --blocker` with proof. Do not route around the contract; do not rewrite criteria.
- An active prerequisite download, installation, remote job, or generation is not a completed work package and is not a reason to end the round with setup receipts. Poll it within the session until it produces the criterion result or a proved terminal failure. When independent accessible work exists, start or continue that work instead of waiting for every dependency behind a common barrier.
- Do not spend turns on cleanup, docs, tooling, or abstractions unless a criterion or blocker requires them.
